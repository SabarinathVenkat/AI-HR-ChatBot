# RMG tailoring playbook

Read this when you reach "Tailor" in SKILL.md. It is the "how we tailor" part: the same habits the Ideas2IT resume builder
follows.

## Order

- Put the projects that fit the JD best first. Within a project, put the bullets that fit the JD best first.
- In the skills section, put the skills the JD asks for first, but only skills the resume really shows. Remove duplicates
  ("Java" and "java" are one skill). Keep the candidate's own groups where you can.

## Wording

- Start bullets with a strong verb. Lead with what was built and the result, then the tools.
- One idea per bullet, one or two lines. Cut filler ("responsible for", "worked on").
- Use the JD's words for things the resume already does ("REST APIs", not "web services") when they mean the same thing.
  Never use a JD word for something the resume does not show.
- Keep every number, date and name exactly as written.

## The summary

- Three or four sentences. Lead with what the JD asks for most, where the resume really shows it.
- Do not state years of experience unless the original summary does.
- Never mention a skill from the gaps list.

## Key highlights

- At least five short statements near the top. Each one must be built from lines the resume already has (you may combine two
  lines about the same kind of work). If the resume has fewer than five, write the rest that way.
- Deep match: eight.

## Bullet counts

- Polish: at most six bullets per project, and each original bullet is used once.
- Deep match: at most eight per project. One original line may feed more than one bullet, and one bullet may draw on several
  lines, but never say the same thing twice.

## Certifications, education, dates

- Certifications section only if the resume has certifications. Do not add any.
- Project start and end dates only if the resume has them.
- Education and employers are copied, not reworded.

## Gaps

- A gap is a JD skill (must-have or nice-to-have) that the resume does not show.
- Write each gap as a question: "Have you worked with Kafka? If yes, in which project?"
- If the candidate answers yes, RMG tells you the projects; then it is "confirmed by the candidate" and you may write it in
  those projects only, as their own statement, and say so in the note.

## Length

- Aim for the template's page count. Never more than 10 pages. Do not shrink fonts to fit; cut the least relevant bullets.
