> Illustrative sample policy written for demonstration.

## 1. Work Model
1.1 The standard model is **hybrid**, with employees working from the office **3 days a week** and
from home on the remaining working days, unless the project or client requires otherwise.
1.2 The days in office are agreed within each team and communicated to the team at least a month
ahead.
1.3 Fully remote work is an exception that needs the written approval of the department head and
HR.

## 2. Work From Home Beyond the Standard Model
2.1 Additional WFH days need prior approval from the project manager, requested at least 1 working
day ahead except in emergencies.
2.2 An employee may request up to **10 additional WFH days per year** under 2.1 for personal
reasons, such as repairs at home or caring responsibilities.
2.3 WFH is not available during the first **30 days** after joining, to support onboarding.
2.4 Working from a different city or country needs approval from HR and the department head, for
tax, legal and data security reasons. Working from abroad is generally not permitted.

## 3. Working Hours
3.1 Standard working hours are **9 hours a day, 5 days a week** (Monday to Friday), including a
1 hour break.
3.2 Teams use core overlap hours of **11:00 to 16:00 IST**, when everyone should be reachable.
3.3 Employees on client projects may need to adjust hours to overlap with client time zones, as
agreed with their manager.

## 4. Attendance Recording
4.1 Office attendance is recorded through badge entry. WFH days are recorded by marking
attendance on the HR portal by 10:30 IST.
4.2 Three late arrivals of more than 30 minutes in a month count as half a day of leave, unless
the manager approves a waiver.

## 5. Overtime and Weekend Work
Overtime is not paid for salaried employees. Approved weekend or holiday work earns Compensatory
Off under POL-02 §2.

## 6. Equipment and Home Setup
The company provides a laptop and accessories. A one-time home office allowance applies as
described in POL-07 §4.
