## 2. Holidays Falling on Weekends
Independence Day (15 August 2026) falls on a Saturday, and no substitute holiday is given. A
holiday that falls on a weekend is not replaced unless HR announces otherwise.

## 3. Floating Holidays
Each employee may choose **2 floating holidays** per calendar year from the list of regional and
religious days published by HR in January. Floating holidays must be requested at least 5 working
days in advance and approved by the manager. They are not leave, do not reduce any leave balance,
and lapse if unused on 31 December.

## 4. Working on a Holiday
An employee asked to work on a public holiday earns one Compensatory Off day (POL-02 §2), valid
for 60 days.

## 5. Other Locations
The Dallas office follows the US federal holiday calendar. Employees on client sites follow the
client's calendar only when the client contract requires it and HR has confirmed it in writing.
