> Illustrative sample policy written for demonstration. Not Ideas2IT's actual policy.

## 1. Leave Year and Scope
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. This policy applies to all full-time employees
in India. Contractors and interns are covered only where stated.

## 2. Leave Types and Annual Entitlement
| Leave type | Code | Entitlement per leave year | Accrual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Leave | AL | 18 days | 1.5 days per month |
| Casual Leave | CL | 6 days | Credited in full on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners) |
| Sick Leave | SL | 8 days | Credited in full on 1 January (pro-rated for joiners) |
| Maternity Leave | ML | 26 weeks | Event based, see §6.1 |
| Paternity Leave | PL | 10 working days | Event based, see §6.2 |
| Bereavement Leave | BL | 5 working days per event | Event based, see §6.3 |
| Marriage Leave | MRL | 5 working days, once in service | Event based |
| Compensatory Off | CO | 1 day per approved weekend or holiday working day | Expires after 60 days |
| Loss of Pay | LOP | Unpaid | When no paid balance is available |

## 3. Accrual and Pro-Rating
3.1 Annual Leave accrues at 1.5 days at the end of each completed month.
3.2 A joiner who joins on or before the 15th of a month receives that month's accrual in full. A
joiner who joins after the 15th receives accrual starting the following month.
3.3 CL and SL for the joining year are pro-rated by the number of remaining months in the leave
year, counting the joining month using the rule in 3.2, and rounded up to the nearest half day.
3.4 Leave balances are shown in days. Half-day leave counts as 0.5 days.

## 4. Eligibility and Rules for Taking Leave
4.1 **Probation.** During probation (see POL-05 §1) an employee may use only leave already accrued.
Negative balances are not allowed. Paternity and Marriage leave are available only after
confirmation. Maternity leave follows the statutory rules regardless of probation.
4.2 **Notice.** Annual Leave of up to 2 days needs 1 working day notice. 3 to 5 days needs 5
working days notice. More than 5 days needs 10 working days notice. Casual and Sick leave may be
taken at short notice.
4.3 **Maximum consecutive Annual Leave** is 15 calendar days without written approval from the
department head.
4.4 **Sick Leave** of more than 2 consecutive working days needs a medical certificate, submitted
within 3 working days of returning.
4.5 **Casual Leave** cannot be combined with Annual Leave for more than 3 consecutive days in
total.
4.6 **Approval.** All leave requires approval from the reporting manager. Approval or rejection
should happen within 2 working days. A request not actioned in 2 working days escalates to the
department head.
4.7 **Insufficient balance.** If balance is insufficient, the excess days are treated as LOP and
the employee must confirm this before the request is submitted.
4.8 **Blackout periods.** The manager may restrict Annual Leave during critical project
milestones, with at least 15 days advance communication to the team.

## 5. Calculating Leave Days
5.1 Leave is counted in **working days**. Weekends (Saturday and Sunday) and company public
holidays (POL-03) that fall inside a leave period are **not** counted as leave days.
5.2 A leave request that starts on a working day and ends on a working day is counted as the
number of working days between them, inclusive.
5.3 Half-day leave is available for the first or second half of a working day and counts as 0.5.
5.4 Example: Annual Leave from Monday to Friday, with a public holiday on Wednesday, counts as
4 days.
5.5 Example: Leave from Friday to the following Monday counts as 2 days (Friday and Monday).
5.6 Floating holidays (POL-03 §3) are not leave and do not reduce any leave balance.

## 6. Event-Based Leave
6.1 **Maternity Leave.** 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for the
third child onwards, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Requires 8 weeks notice where
possible. Up to 12 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date.
6.2 **Paternity Leave.** 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or
adoption.
6.3 **Bereavement Leave.** 5 working days on the death of a parent, spouse, child, sibling or
parent-in-law. Short notice is allowed.
6.4 **Marriage Leave.** 5 working days, once during service, applied at least 15 days in advance.

## 7. Carry Forward and Encashment
7.1 Up to **10 days** of unused Annual Leave carry forward to the next leave year. The excess
lapses on 31 December.
7.2 Casual Leave and Sick Leave do not carry forward and are not encashable.
7.3 Accumulated Annual Leave (including carry forward) is capped at **30 days**.
7.4 Annual Leave is encashed only at separation, up to the available balance, at basic salary
divided by 26 per day. Encashment of balance during employment is not allowed.
7.5 Compensatory Off expires 60 days after it is granted and is not encashable.

## 8. Loss of Pay
8.1 Unapproved absence or leave beyond the available balance is treated as LOP.
8.2 LOP per day is calculated as monthly gross salary divided by the number of calendar days in
the month.
8.3 Absence without notice for 3 consecutive working days may be treated as job abandonment and
handled under POL-08.

## 9. Leave Records
Leave applications, approvals and balances are recorded in the HR system. Balances shown by the HR
assistant are read from this system. If the assistant's balance differs from a payslip or manager
records, contact HR for correction.
