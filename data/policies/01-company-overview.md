> Items marked **[Public]** come from ideas2it.com. All other content is an illustrative sample.

## 1. About the Company
Ideas2IT is a software and AI consulting company headquartered in Dallas, Texas, with a major
engineering presence in Chennai, India. [Public] It reports more than 15 years in operation,
700+ engineers and 200+ clients. [Public]

## 2. Values
Our values are **Freedom with Responsibility**, **Employees First** and **Empowerment**. The guiding
philosophy is "Lofty Goals, Happy Journey". [Public]

## 3. Learning and Career Growth
Employees receive mentorship, tailored training and career mapping. [Public] Illustrative detail:
each employee agrees a development plan with their manager at the start of the appraisal cycle
(see POL-06 §3) and may use the learning budget described in POL-07 §5.

## 4. Hiring Process
The process has four steps: resume or portfolio review, a real-world task, a conversation with team
members, then a quick decision. [Public]

## 5. Equal Opportunity
The company is committed to a diverse and inclusive workplace. [Public] Illustrative detail:
hiring, pay and promotion decisions are made without regard to gender, religion, caste, age,
disability, sexual orientation or marital status.

## 6. Employee Referral (Illustrative)
Employees may refer candidates through the HR portal. A referral bonus of INR 25,000 is paid
after the referred employee completes 6 months of service. Not applicable to HR team members or
the hiring manager.
