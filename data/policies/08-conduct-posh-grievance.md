## 1. Code of Conduct
1.1 Employees are expected to act with integrity, respect colleagues and clients, and follow the
law.
1.2 Conflicts of interest, such as holding a second job with a client or competitor, must be
disclosed to HR in writing. Moonlighting is not permitted without written approval.
1.3 Accepting gifts from clients or vendors worth more than **INR 3,000** must be reported to HR.

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
2.1 The company has zero tolerance for sexual harassment, in line with the POSH Act, 2013.
2.2 Complaints may be made to the **Internal Committee (IC)** in writing within 3 months of the
incident, extendable by another 3 months for good reason.
2.3 Complaints are handled confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who complains is itself
misconduct.
2.4 IC contact (illustrative): ic@example-ideas2it.invalid

## 3. Grievance Redressal
3.1 Step 1: raise the issue with the reporting manager. Step 2: raise it with HR through the HR
portal or hr@example-ideas2it.invalid. Step 3: escalate to the Head of HR.
3.2 HR acknowledges grievances within **2 working days** and aims to resolve them within
**15 working days**.
3.3 Employees may raise concerns anonymously through the ethics line (illustrative).

## 4. Disciplinary Process
4.1 For alleged misconduct the company issues a written show-cause notice and allows **5 working
days** to respond.
4.2 An inquiry follows where needed. Outcomes range from a warning to termination, in proportion
to the misconduct.

## 5. Information Security
5.1 Company and client data must be handled only on company-approved devices and systems.
5.2 Laptops must have disk encryption and screen lock enabled.
5.3 Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team immediately.
5.4 Using public AI tools with confidential client data is not permitted.

## 6. Whistleblower Protection
Good-faith reports of suspected wrongdoing are protected, and the reporter's identity is kept
confidential as far as the law allows.
