> Illustrative sample policy written for demonstration.

## 1. General Rules
1.1 Only business expenses incurred with prior manager approval are reimbursable.
1.2 Claims must be submitted on the HR portal with original bills within **30 days** of the
expense. Claims older than 30 days need department head approval.
1.3 Claims are approved by the manager, then Finance, and paid with the next payroll if approved
before the cut-off (POL-06 §1.3).

## 2. Travel
2.1 Domestic travel: economy air or AC train, and a hotel up to **INR 6,000 per night** in metro
cities and **INR 4,500** elsewhere.
2.2 Daily meal allowance during travel is **INR 800**.
2.3 Local conveyance for business travel is reimbursed against receipts.

## 3. Client Entertainment
Client entertainment needs prior approval from the department head and is capped at **INR 2,500
per person per event**.

## 4. Home Office Allowance
A one-time home office allowance of **INR 10,000** is available after confirmation for ergonomic
equipment such as a chair or monitor, against bills. It may be claimed once every 3 years.

## 5. Learning and Certification Budget
5.1 Each employee has a learning budget of **INR 30,000 per leave year** for courses, books and
certifications relevant to their role.
5.2 Certifications above INR 30,000 need approval from the department head.
5.3 Reimbursement is paid after successful completion. If an employee resigns within **12 months**
of receiving reimbursement, the amount must be repaid on a pro-rated basis.

## 6. Mobile and Internet
Employees in roles that need connectivity receive **INR 1,000 per month** for internet, taxable as
per rules, claimed monthly with a bill.

## 7. Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal expenses, fines, alcohol, personal insurance and expenses without a bill are not
reimbursable.
