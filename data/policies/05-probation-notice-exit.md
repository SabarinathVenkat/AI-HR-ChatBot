> Illustrative sample policy written for demonstration.

## 1. Probation
1.1 New employees serve a probation period of **6 months** from the date of joining.
1.2 Probation may be extended once, by up to 3 months, if performance expectations are not met.
The employee is told in writing, with reasons and improvement goals.
1.3 During probation, either party may end employment with **15 days** notice.
1.4 Leave rules during probation are in POL-02 §4.1.

## 2. Confirmation
2.1 A confirmation review takes place in the last month of probation, led by the reporting manager
with HR.
2.2 Confirmation is communicated in writing. If no communication is received, the employee should
contact HR; probation is not confirmed automatically by silence.

## 3. Notice Period
3.1 After confirmation the notice period is **60 days** for individual contributors and **90 days**
for managers and above.
3.2 Notice must be submitted in writing through the HR portal to the reporting manager.
3.3 Leave taken during notice period extends the notice period by the number of leave days taken,
except for Sick Leave supported by a medical certificate.

## 4. Notice Buyout and Early Release
4.1 The company may agree to an early release against a buyout, subject to manager and HR approval
and business needs.
4.2 Buyout is calculated as gross monthly salary divided by 30, multiplied by the number of notice
days not served.
4.3 The company may also waive part of the notice period at its discretion.

## 5. Exit Process
5.1 **Handover.** A handover plan is agreed within 5 working days of resignation being accepted.
5.2 **Exit interview** is held in the last week of service with HR.
5.3 **Clearance.** The employee returns laptop, access cards and other company assets and obtains
no-dues clearance from IT, Finance and the manager.
5.4 **Full and final settlement** is processed within **45 days** of the last working day and
includes pending salary, leave encashment under POL-02 §7.4, and deductions for any dues.
5.5 **Documents.** The relieving letter and experience letter are issued after full and final
settlement, within 7 working days of clearance.

## 6. Bench and Performance Improvement (Illustrative)
6.1 Employees between projects ("on bench") continue to receive full pay. Bench time is used for
training and internal work. Prolonged bench time beyond **90 days** may trigger a role review.
6.2 A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasts **60 days**, with written goals, regular check-ins
and a documented outcome. The employee may add comments to the final review.

## 7. Termination
7.1 Termination for misconduct follows the process in POL-08, including a written show-cause
notice and a chance to respond.
7.2 Absence without notice for 3 consecutive working days may be treated as abandonment after HR
attempts to contact the employee.
