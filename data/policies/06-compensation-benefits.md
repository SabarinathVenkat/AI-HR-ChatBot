> Illustrative sample policy written for demonstration. Public sources mention generous paid time
> off, parental, sick and bereavement leave, an Employee Assistance Program and a tax benefit
> card; the figures below are illustrative.

## 1. Payroll
1.1 Salary is paid on the **last working day of each month** by bank transfer.
1.2 Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month.
1.3 Payroll cut-off for attendance and leave is the **25th** of each month. Changes after the
cut-off apply to the next month.
1.4 Payroll queries should be sent to payroll within 5 working days of the payslip being issued.

## 2. Statutory Deductions
2.1 **Provident Fund (PF).** Employee and employer each contribute 12% of basic salary, subject to
statutory limits.
2.2 **Professional tax and income tax (TDS)** are deducted as per applicable law. Employees submit
investment declarations in April and proofs in January.
2.3 **Gratuity** is payable after 5 years of continuous service, as per the Payment of Gratuity Act.

## 3. Performance Appraisal
3.1 The appraisal cycle runs from **1 April to 31 March**, with goals set in April.
3.2 A mid-year check-in takes place in October and a final review in April.
3.3 Ratings use a 5-point scale. Increments and promotions are effective **1 May**, subject to
business performance.
3.4 Employees confirmed after 1 October of the cycle are reviewed in the following cycle.
3.5 Employees on a PIP at the time of review are not eligible for an increment until the PIP is
closed successfully.

## 4. Health and Wellbeing
4.1 **Group medical insurance** covers the employee, spouse, two children and, optionally, parents
at an additional cost. Employee cover is INR 5 lakh, effective from the date of joining.
4.2 **Group term life and accident cover** is provided at 3 times annual fixed pay, up to a limit.
4.3 **Employee Assistance Program (EAP).** Confidential counselling is available at no cost to the
employee and family members. Details are on the HR portal.
4.4 **Annual health check-up** is provided for employees aged 30 and above.

## 5. Other Benefits
5.1 **Tax-saving benefit card** for meal and other eligible components, through the company's
benefits partner.
5.2 **Salary advance and personal loan** facility after confirmation, up to 2 months of net salary,
repayable in up to 12 monthly instalments.
5.3 **Learning budget** is described in POL-07 §5.

## 6. Relocation (Illustrative)
Relocation support up to INR 50,000 may be offered for approved inter-city transfers, with
reimbursement against bills under POL-07.
