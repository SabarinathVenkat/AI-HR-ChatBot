Data flow · RAG pipeline

Policy RAG: indexing and cited answers

In plain words

Offline, the policy files are split by section heading, each section becomes one chunk with its policy ID, version, effective date and clause ids, and each chunk is embedded locally with bge-small. Text, keyword index and vectors live in the same SQLite file.

Online, a question searches keywords and vectors in parallel, takes the top 50 of each, and merges them by reciprocal rank fusion into the top five. There is no reranker, because with about 50 sections it was not needed. The best vector similarity then sets one of three tiers: confident (0.655 or more), weak match (0.50 to 0.655, up to three sections, flagged), or nothing (below 0.50), in which case the agent says it could not find the answer and gives the HR contact.

The model writes citations such as POL-02 §5.4 as plain text, and the verifier later checks that each citation was really retrieved in that turn.
